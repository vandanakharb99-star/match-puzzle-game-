📜 Privacy Policy
Last Updated: June 9, 2026

Introduction
Welcome to Puzzle Match Pi ("Game," "we," "our," "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you play our browser-based match-3 puzzle game and use related services (collectively, the "Services").

Information We Collect
1. Pi Network Authentication Data
When you sign in with Pi Network to play Puzzle Match Pi, we receive:

Your Pi Network username

Your Pi Network user ID (UID)

An authentication token (access token)

We do not store your private keys, wallet passphrase, or any financial information.

2. Game Progress Data
We save your game progress locally on your device, including:

Current level

Total score

Level completion status

This data is stored in your browser's local storage and is never transmitted to our servers.

3. Usage Data
We may automatically collect anonymous information about how you interact with the Game, such as:

Device type and browser version

Game actions (matches made, levels completed)

Time spent playing

4. Cookies and Local Storage
We use browser local storage and cookies to:

Save your game progress between sessions

Remember your Pi Network authentication session

Store your game preferences

How We Use Your Information
1. To Provide and Improve the Game
We use your Pi Network authentication to:

Identify you as a unique player

Save your game progress across sessions

Personalize your gaming experience

2. Game Analytics
We use anonymous usage data to:

Understand how players interact with the game

Improve game balance and difficulty progression

Fix bugs and enhance performance

3. Security
We use authentication data to:

Prevent fraud and unauthorized access

Protect player accounts and game progress

Sharing Your Information
1. Pi Network
We share your authentication token with Pi Network's API (https://api.minepi.com/v2/me) solely to verify your identity. No other data is shared.

2. Service Providers
We may use third-party analytics services that collect anonymous usage data. These providers are contractually obligated to protect your information.

3. Legal Requirements
We may disclose your information if required by law or in response to valid legal requests.

Data Storage and Security
Your Game Progress is stored locally on your device using your browser's local storage. We do not have access to this data on our servers.

Pi Network Authentication tokens are stored locally and used only to verify your identity with Pi Network's official API.

Security Measures We Implement:

HTTPS encryption for all communications

No storage of sensitive Pi Network credentials

Regular security reviews

However, please note that no method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Your Choices and Rights
1. Sign Out
You can sign out of Puzzle Match Pi at any time using the sign-out button in the game. This will clear your authentication session.

2. Clear Game Data
You can clear all your game progress at any time by:

Using the "NEW" button in the game (resets current level)

Clearing your browser's local storage and cookies

3. Delete Your Data
Since all game data is stored locally, you can delete everything by:

Clearing your browser's local storage

Using your browser's "Clear Site Data" feature

4. Cookies
You can set your browser to refuse cookies or alert you when cookies are being sent. However, disabling cookies may prevent the game from saving your progress.

Children's Privacy
Puzzle Match Pi is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us.

International Data Transfers
Your information may be processed on servers located outside your country. By using Puzzle Match Pi, you consent to this transfer.

Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by:

Posting the new Privacy Policy in the game

Updating the "Last Updated" date

We encourage you to review this Privacy Policy periodically.

Third-Party Links
Puzzle Match Pi may contain links to third-party websites (e.g., Pi Network). We are not responsible for the privacy practices of these third parties.

Contact Us
If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or Puzzle Match Pi, you can contact Article 19 Group through:

Pi Network Developer Community – Search for "Puzzle Match Pi"

GitHub Issues – Report privacy concerns through our project repository
